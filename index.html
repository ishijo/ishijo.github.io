Hey, this is Ishika Johari! 👋

Data scientist working at the intersection of behavioral science, personalization, and AI.

About Me

I’m drawn to the space where behavioral science meets data — the correlation maps that tell you what actually drives a choice, the customer journeys hiding inside a funnel, the moment you realize this is why people came back and that is why they didn’t.

I’m Ishika. I spent nearly three years at Dell Technologies on the customer experimentation team, where I built recommender systems, NLP tooling, and Tia Insights Advisor (TIA) — a self-serve chatbot that made experimentation insights searchable across the org, patented in 2024. I’ve also spoken at Kaggle Days as an invited webinar speaker on experimentation fundamentals.

I recently finished my MS in Information Management at the University of Washington, working as a Graduate Research Scholar at the eScience Institute on large-scale astronomy tools with Caltech and Rice, and my capstone with Google.

Lately I’ve been using AI to accelerate all of this — and rebuilding older ideas with it, just to see what actually changes. My Experimentation Intelligence Agent (EIA) is the AI-native successor to TIA: same problem (surfacing experimentation insights on demand), new substrate.

Off-hours: psychology, music, singing, and reading 🎧 Reach out through the socials on the left.

Experimentation Intelligence Agent

In active development

An agentic RAG system for natural language querying of historical A/B test results, with built-in bias detection for experiment validity. Multi-node LangGraph pipeline over a ChromaDB vector store of experiment metadata; local-first LLM inference with hosted-API fallback for production.

LangGraph · LlamaIndex · ChromaDB · sentence-transformers · FastAPI · Ollama · Claude API

Repo coming soon

Travel Recommender and Itinerary Generator

Github

A Travel Itinerary generator customised to the user's vacation type, budget and other details. The Itinerary suggests possible places the user would like to visit tailored to the available timings. This project considers only Jaipur, India as the destination city at the moment.

Cross Content Recommender

Github

Cross-content recommender matching 18K+ books to movies using SBERT embeddings and contrastive learning (InfoNCE), with LLM-powered genre mapping across 461 book genres and FAISS indexing for real-time retrieval. Self-scraped datasets from Goodreads and IMDB.

SBERT · FAISS · Contrastive Learning · Claude API · Streamlit · FastAPI
